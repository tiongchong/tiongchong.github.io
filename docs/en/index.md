# Benchkeeper

Engineer, Builder, Husband & Father — Penang, Malaysia

I spend my days working close to the metal and the stack—from low-level hardware and firmware to embedded Linux, edge AI validation, and automated test pipelines—and my evenings and weekends learning, mentoring, and caring for a busy household of seven.

Over more than two decades across electrical R&D, FPGA and IP engineering, bare-metal firmware, software validation, and running a workshop in fine art framing, restoration, and precision woodworking, I have come to value simple principles: test what you build, understand how systems behave under real constraints, and keep your work honest. Outside of work, I enjoy tinkering with microcontrollers, software-defined radio, and self-hosted tools, as well as working with my hands in wood and craft.

Most importantly, my life is anchored in Christian faith. This site is a quiet notebook rather than a polished stage. Here I gather:

- [Faith & Reflections](faith/index.md) — notes from daily devotion, Scripture, and structured prayer.

- [Books I Read](reading/index.md) — takeaways from history, biography, character, and thoughtful non-fiction as I read and share notes over time.

- [Projects & Craft](projects/index.md) — practical notes from engineering experiments, technical validation, and hands-on making.

Nothing here claims to have all the answers. It is simply a record of what I am learning, building, and holding fast to along the way—shared in three languages so family, friends, and fellow travelers can read in the language that feels most like home.
